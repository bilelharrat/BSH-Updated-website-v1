Our story

It started with a mattress, a chair and a lamp.

A mother bought a white house in the Berkeley Hills for her daughter. They offered a room to a young founder who had arrived with a one-way ticket and an AI startup idea. Within days, the walls were covered in ideas.

So an idea sparked: why not turn the house into a platform, where more founders could dream, build and grow?
