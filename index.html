Origin story

It began with a house that was waiting.

Berkeley Hills, 2024

Our story began in a white house in the Berkeley Hills, a place of wonder purchased by a mother for her daughter, meant to be more than just a house, but whose purpose was yet to come. For a while, the house sat empty, waiting…

One day, they met a young founder who arrived in the United States with nothing but a one-way ticket and an AI startup idea. They welcomed him with open arms and offered him a place to stay. A few days later, when they walked into the house, what they saw struck them.

The room was almost empty, just a mattress, a chair, and a lamp. But the walls were completely covered with scribbles of ideas, diagrams, and thoughts. A buzz and energy filled the room. It was as if the space had exploded with imagination.

In that moment, an idea sparked: Why not transform the house into a platform, a place where more founders like him could dream, build, and grow? With this idea, Berkeley Summit House was born in 2024.

The world is full of people with incredible potential who are missing just one thing: a chance to be believed in.

They need a space where they are free to explore, stumble, and grow. So, we gave this house a new life. Today, it has become an integral part of our community, unlocking entrepreneurs’ boundless potential and driving impactful innovation.
