Where we build

Four essential services, opened up by AI.

We co-develop consumer-first products that bridge the gap in technology access, so what used to be scarce becomes available to everyone.

  • Education

    We transform learning through personalized, adaptive AI. We co-develop accessible knowledge systems that democratize high-quality education for students everywhere.

  • Wellbeing

    We expand access to essential support through AI companions and wellness platforms. We co-develop tools that provide personalized, on-demand care, making mental well-being a reality for all.

  • Legal

    We empower individuals to navigate the law with AI-driven agents. We co-develop accessible tools to understand rights and complex procedures, breaking down the financial barriers to justice.

  • Arts

    We expand the horizon of creative expression through AI-driven tools. We co-develop platforms that augment human talent, making sophisticated content creation accessible to every creator.

How we work

Side by side, not at arm’s length.

  • Engage deeply

    We work with founders on the product itself, in the same room when we can, rather than advising from a distance.

  • Bridge capability gaps

    A young team rarely has every skill it needs. We help close the gaps in expertise, networks and resources.

  • Build market-ready

    The aim is AI people can use and afford today: consumer-first, accessible, and ready for the market.
