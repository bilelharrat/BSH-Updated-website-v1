Our Story

It started with a house.

A white house in the Berkeley Hills, a founder with a one-way ticket, and walls covered in ideas.

Our story began in a white house in the Berkeley Hills, bought by a mother for her daughter. It was meant to be more than a house, but its purpose was yet to come. So it waited.

One day, they met a young founder who had arrived in the United States with nothing but a one-way ticket and an AI startup idea. They offered him a place to stay.

A few days later, the room held a mattress, a chair and a lamp. The walls held everything else: ideas, diagrams and thoughts, as if the space had exploded with imagination.

So an idea sparked. Why not turn the house into a platform, where more founders could dream, build and grow?
